# Universal Randomizer 1.7.0 — Gen 1 world shuffle

Update through the launcher/mod manager, fully restart, and start a new Red, Blue or Yellow run for the new shuffles. Gold/Silver/Crystal stay on their released behavior.

- New **Gyms + entrances** settings: native gym leaders, gym doors and audited dungeon entrances, with Off/Safe/Chaos choices; separate or mixed pools and independent or leader-paired badges.
- Expanded joint item/badge/story/entrance proof, exact paired returns, one-way route boundaries and dungeon HM requirements. Constructive Safe generation keeps a proven layout and validates the final placement independently.
- Frame-staged validation with a hard **50-candidate limit** and visible attempt counts. An unproven Safe seed is never accepted. Chaos can still be unbeatable; missing or unsupported data fails instead of bypassing checks.
- Safe shuffled worlds use earned-badge encounter and boss ceilings, including when Random Start is Off. Native lower levels and your percentage settings are respected.
- Saved entrance journal, factual progression tracker, area/exact hints, and a bogus roast in every four new rumors.
- Optional first-victory / first-capture ordinary item bonuses. Claims survive reloading and rematches; full bags send rewards to the PC.
- Native portraits and overworld sprites; original gym completion flags and TM rewards. Continue verifies its saved mapping and restores presentation immediately.

The proof covers modeled native progression, assuming winnable combat, available renewable money and use of earned HMs. It does not certify arbitrary quest mods or all player decisions. Ten audited dungeon components are supported; internal floors, Safari/ship/forest/Rocket entrances and special transitions remain native. No full Android/ROM playthrough was performed.

See `docs/AUDIT-1.7.0.md` for sources, tests and limitations.
