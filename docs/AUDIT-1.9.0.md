# Adaptive gym team audit — 1.9.0

## Scope and sources

Optional badge-driven gym teams for all eight Gen 1 and sixteen Gen 2 leaders. The native host is pinned to Gen1Recomp 0.3.99 (`8b981540e882440f33ab09f1f56030e0ce4a8787`). `tools/adaptive_native_references.json` pins 832 source-file hashes across public pret/pokered, pokeyellow, pokegold and pokecrystal revisions. `tools/adaptive_native_fixture.py` rebuilds the committed fixture of native species, moves, evolution families, learnability and trainer rosters and compares it exactly. Red/Blue and Gold/Silver share their respective native combat registries; Yellow and Crystal have separate records. No ROM or artwork is included in the test data.

## Construction and precedence

The opt-in control is independent of world Safe/Chaos policy. Safe teams have two members at 0–1 badges, then one more per badge to six at five. Basic forms are required before two badges; intermediate forms are eligible at two; final forms at four, subject to native evolution level and strength ceilings. Item/happiness evolutions have a level-20 floor, trades level 30. Gen 2 BST comparisons average the split special stats to use the same five-stat difficulty budget as Gen 1. Original evolution relationships are retained independently of randomized evolution targets.

Every member matches the current leader's type using its seeded types; Blue is mixed. At least one eligible member of a native roster family is selected when available. Other choices prefer stronger eligible stages and families, without repeats until the eligible pool is exhausted. Small pools can repeat a species. Legendaries follow the existing toggle. Safe damage budgets are 60/80/100/150 effective base power at 0/2/4/6 badges, accounting for multi-hit upper bounds. Accuracy must be at least 70. Fixed damage, OHKO, self-destruct, unpredictable damage and early Gen 1 trapping are excluded. Curated utility moves expand from simple debuffs to coverage, screens, status, recovery and setup. A usable damaging move is required; usable legal STAB is preferred where possible. No synthetic move is added for types lacking one. Chaos retains legal native pools but removes stage/strength/move-power limits and rolls two to six members.

The ace level is 12 + 4 per earned badge through eight, then 5 per additional badge in Gen 2. Other members are at most two levels lower. Boss percentage is applied once afterward, clamped to 1–100; it changes levels without rerolling composition. Gen 2 held Berries start at four badges and Leftovers at six when present in native items. Gen 1 receives no held items. Native AI and selected AI settings still own move decisions; no new battle engine is installed.

The existing final trainer-party hook supplies adaptive gym parties after ordinary species and level mutations. Only exact native gym identities qualify, including Giovanni's third Gen 1 party and the Gen 2 numeric/string trainer identifiers. Ordinary trainers, rivals and non-gym Rocket Giovanni retain existing behavior. Adaptive gym species/moves supersede trainer species, BST limits, learnsets and randomized TM compatibility. Species stats/types remain seeded. Gen 1's post-construction signature-move and moved-leader presentation paths preserve the explicit adaptive moves. Gen 2 uses native `Mon.new` and the battle constructor's stat refresh. Original gym events, completion keys, badges/TMs and native portraits remain under the world adapter.

## Validation and saves

Before an adaptive candidate is accepted, all native gym rosters must exist and every leader must have an eligible team at every badge count, 0–8 or 0–16. Empty themed pools return a seed failure to the existing frame-staged, at-most-50-candidate generator; no team restriction is silently disabled. The existing world solver still proves its authored item/badge/entrance route independently. Combat feasibility is bounded, not solved. Unsafe arbitrary external data can still make a configuration unsatisfiable.

The seed, source gym, moved leader, earned badge count and adaptive mode determine each team. Continue reconstructs it from the saved configuration and certified world; rematches at the same badge count do not reroll. Existing saves normalize to Off. Off is omitted from the fingerprint input, preserving 1.8.0 run codes, fingerprints and placements. Enabled runs include an explicit adaptive schema marker and UR190 namespace.

## Checks and limits

`tests/adaptive_gyms.lua` exercises 6,192 native team plans across all six editions, every leader, every badge count, both modes and three seeds. It checks native pool boundaries, themes, safe opening stages and moves, move legality/PP, legendary exclusion, deterministic repeats and badge aliases. Actual Gen 1 and Gen 2 battle constructors run with and without combined leader/entrance/Random Start shuffling, at multiple badge counts. Checks cover final HP/stats, explicit moves, held items, native identity, percentage precedence and untouched owned moves/PP. Twenty-four combined configurations exercise randomized stats/types/evolutions/learnsets/TMs, zero BST tolerance and independent world safety. Eighteen released 1.8.0 configurations are compared through both new generation and cold Continue; retained 1.7.0 migrations also run.

The release workflow runs the complete packaged feature, item, start, native-content, encounter, menu, settings/progression, Type Bias, Gen 1/2 world and release-regression suites under LuaJIT and Lua 5.4. Actual LOVE rendering checks 34 menu views and font bounds, including both new mode guides and their scroll endpoints. The prior pinned Untamed compatibility and encounter-level tests remain included.

These checks do not constitute a full ROM/Android playthrough or a proof that every battle can be won. Safe controls team construction; player party choices, high Boss percentages, native AI items and external gameplay mods can still change difficulty. The earlier progression proof retains its authored model and assumptions. Chaos is explicitly unrestricted and is not certified beatable.
