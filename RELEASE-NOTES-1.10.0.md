# Universal Randomizer 1.10.0

Requires launcher **0.3.99+**. Update through the launcher mod manager, then fully restart. Gen 1 and Gen 2 keep native Pokemon, rules and default sprites.

- **Adaptive story bosses:** Safe / Chaos for rivals, Giovanni, Yellow's Jessie & James, Rocket executives, Elite Four, Champions and Gen 2 Red. Safe protects opening rival fights and follows earned badges and native story phase. Gym teams remain a separate control.
- **Run tools:** three editable presets with change previews; Champion, all-badge and deterministic checklist goals; Gen 2 Red goal; spoiler-hiding race mode.
- **Phone/PC overlays:** move and resize five optional panels with touch or mouse, plus controller/virtual-pad editing. Save separate portrait/landscape layouts. Panels cover run/splits, party/EXP, caught/native-shiny status, battle moves and move-learning comparisons. Tap move panels for full scrolling details.
- **Share codes:** export every setting and the accepted seed; clipboard import validates checksum, cartridge and settings before a change preview. Exact imported seeds are never silently replaced.
- **Run recap and diagnostics:** saved clock, splits, discoveries, key/HM observations, catches, blackouts, finish team and post-goal bogus-hint reveal. Copy a reproducible diagnostic report locally.
- **Touch menus:** tap rows and footer controls; swipe through settings or long guides. A drag or cancelled gesture cannot activate a setting.

New gameplay controls default Off / Native ending. Existing seeds and 1.9.0 adaptive gym plans keep their previous behavior. Start a new run for new gameplay settings. HUD layouts are editable during a run; panels start Off to avoid overlapping other installed HUDs. Older saves begin recording from their first load in this version.

Custom goals use the existing full progression proof, including keys/HMs, badge dependencies and shuffled entrances. Safe seed generation stays bounded to 50 attempts and rejects failed proofs. Access proofs assume manageable combat and resources; Chaos permits locks. Race mode is spoiler protection, not anti-cheat.

Open **New run setup → Run tools** for presets, goals and import. Open **Rando Info → Run tools** for overlays, progress, sharing, recap and diagnostics. Story teams are under **Battles → Adaptive story bosses**.

Automated validation covers the native six-game corpus, real Gen 1/2 battle constructors, save/Continue, published-version comparisons, gestures and actual LOVE UI renders. No physical Android playthrough is claimed. See `docs/AUDIT-1.10.0.md` and `REFERENCE-MODS.md` in the package.
