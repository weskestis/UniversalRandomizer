# Universal Randomizer 1.9.0 — Adaptive gym teams

Update through the launcher/mod manager, fully restart, then start a new run and choose **Gyms + entrances → Adaptive teams: Safe or Chaos**. Available in Red, Blue, Yellow, Gold, Silver and Crystal. Off is the default and preserves existing seeds and team behavior.

- **Safe:** two Pokemon at 0–1 badges, growing to six at five badges. Early basic forms and simple moves; stronger native evolutions, coverage and support moves later.
- **Chaos:** seeded teams of 2–6 without the early evolution, strength or move-power limits.
- Teams follow the active leader's type, including shuffled leaders. Blue uses mixed types. Native evolution families are preferred when eligible; the legendary toggle still applies.
- Levels follow earned badges, including all 16 in Gen 2. Boss level % applies once. Your own party's level cannot increase gym difficulty.
- Uses only the current game's native species, legal native moves and default visuals. Gen 2 can use held items later; Gen 1 cannot.
- Independent combat and world safety controls. A complete scrolling guide explains the mode, level curve and precedence over trainer species, BST and learnset/TM settings for gym opponents.
- Validation checks every leader at every badge count. Native Gen 1 signature moves cannot overwrite an adaptive moveset. Player Pokemon, PP, gym events and badge/TM rewards remain owned by their existing systems.

Tested with native combat data, both actual host battle constructors, shuffled worlds and Random Start, conflicting settings, save/Continue and frozen 1.8.0 comparisons. Safe bounds teams; it does not guarantee winning a battle. No full ROM/Android playthrough was performed. See `docs/AUDIT-1.9.0.md`.
